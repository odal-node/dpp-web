---

Odal Node creates, signs and serves EU Digital Product Passports on your own
infrastructure, with your own keys. Anyone can verify a passport it publishes without
going through Odal. The regulatory library is Apache-2.0; the node is BSL-1.1 and free
to self-host for your own compliance obligations. The project is pre-production.
