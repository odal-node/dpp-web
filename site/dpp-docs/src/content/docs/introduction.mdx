Odal Node is software for creating, signing and serving **EU Digital Product Passports (DPPs)** under the Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation (ESPR). A manufacturer uses it to produce passports that anyone can verify, without handing its production data to a third party.

Each passport also has a history: publication, amendments, transfer of responsibility and end of life. The node records it on a tamper-evident, hash-chained audit trail and can export all of it as a **signed evidence dossier**, which an auditor can verify offline.

## Who it's for

- **Manufacturers and operators** facing an ESPR deadline who need to issue compliant passports and keep control of their data.
- **Developers** who want to run it on their own infrastructure, with no licence keys and no capability caps.
- **Authorities, recyclers and customers** who need to check that a passport is genuine. They check it against the manufacturer's public identity; Odal is not involved.

## How it's organised

Odal Node has two parts, each under its own licence:

| Part | Licence | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| [`dpp-core`](https://github.com/odal-node/dpp-core) | Apache‑2.0 | The regulatory library: the rules a compliant passport must satisfy. |
| [`dpp-engine`](https://github.com/odal-node/dpp-engine) | BSL‑1.1 | The service that runs those rules in production. |

The core holds the rules and the engine applies them. [Core Concepts](/core-concepts) explains why they are split this way: the rules stay open and free, and the operational layer around them is what pays for the project. [Licensing](/getting-started/licensing) covers what each licence allows.
